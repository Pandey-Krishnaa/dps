The School

Everything your child needs, without leaving Hardoi.

Families here have long sent children to Lucknow or Bareilly for a school of this kind. From April 2027 that journey ends — the same curriculum, the same standard, close to home.

The CBSE curriculum

Syllabus, textbooks and assessment follow the CBSE framework, so a transfer anywhere in the country doesn't interrupt a child's schooling.

Purpose-built campus

Nothing retrofitted. Classrooms, labs, library and playing fields are designed together on a single 6-acre site.

Teachers first

Recruitment starts well before opening, so staff complete induction and training before the first child arrives.

Hindi and English, both strong

English as the medium of instruction, with Hindi taught to the same standard rather than as an afterthought.

Transparent fees

One published fee structure, no mid-session revisions, no compulsory purchases beyond uniform and books.

Close to home

Bus routes across the district, so a child's day goes to lessons and games, not to travel.

Our promise to parents

English is for your child. It is not a test of you.

The school teaches in English. But if your own English isn't strong, or you didn't get much schooling yourself — your child loses nothing for it. Teaching English is our work, not yours. You send your child to school; we take care of the rest.

We speak to you in Hindi. Teachers, the office, phone calls, messages — in whichever language you're comfortable with.
Extra English support for children who need it. A child coming from a Hindi-medium school gets help catching up, without being made to feel behind.
Parent meetings are held in Hindi. The report card is explained to you, not just handed over.

Questions

What parents ask first.

Is the school part of a group or society?

No. Delhi Premium School, Hardoi is an independent school run by its own management committee. It is not part of, or affiliated with, any national school chain, franchise or society. The curriculum it teaches is CBSE.

My English isn't good. Will my child struggle here?

No. This is the question we are asked most, and the answer is simple: teaching English is the school's job, not the parent's. We speak to you in Hindi, parent meetings are held in Hindi, and a child arriving from a Hindi-medium school gets extra help with English. How much schooling you had will not decide your child's future here.

When do classes start and when do admissions open?

Classes begin in April 2027 and admission forms open in February 2027. The priority list is open now, and families on it are called first, on the day forms open.

How many students will there be in a class?

Class sizes are published with the prospectus when admissions open in February. We don't quote a figure before it's settled.

What is the fee structure?

The full fee structure — admission fee, term fee, and transport slabs — is published with the prospectus in February and goes to priority-list families first. There are no separate development or capitation charges.

My child is in Class VI elsewhere. Can they transfer?

Yes. Classes I–XI take transfer admissions subject to seats, a transfer certificate from the previous school, and a short placement check so we can put support where it's needed. Class XII transfers follow CBSE's own rules.

Is there an entrance test for Nursery?

No. Admission at Pre-Primary is through a play-based interaction and a conversation with parents. Written testing at this age is not permitted and we don't do it.

Will there be a hostel?

Not in the founding session. The campus is planned as a day school; any residential facility would be a later phase and announced separately.
